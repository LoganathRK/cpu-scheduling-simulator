Round Robin

Preemptive  ·  Time-Sharing  ·  Fair Scheduling

Algorithm Explanation

Round Robin (RR) is designed especially for time-sharing systems. Each process gets a fixed time slice called a Time Quantum (Q).

Processes are placed in a circular queue. Each gets the CPU for up to Q units. If not finished, it is preempted and placed at the end of the queue. This continues until all processes complete.

Key Insight: Smaller quantum improves responsiveness but increases context switch overhead. Larger quantum makes it behave more like FCFS. The ideal quantum balances both.

  • Fair Scheduling
