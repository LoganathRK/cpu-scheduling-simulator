First Come First Served

Non-Preemptive  ·  Simplest CPU Scheduling Algorithm

Algorithm Explanation

First Come First Served (FCFS) is the simplest CPU scheduling algorithm. Processes are executed in the order they arrive in the ready queue — like a queue at a ticket counter.

It is a non-preemptive algorithm: once a process starts executing, it runs to completion without interruption. The CPU is allocated to the process with the earliest arrival time.

Drawback: FCFS can suffer from the "Convoy Effect" — short processes may wait behind long ones, leading to higher average waiting times.

  • Non-Preemptive
  • FIFO Queue
  • Simple to Implement
  • No Starvation
  • Convoy Effect Possible
